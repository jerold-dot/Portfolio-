About Me
Jerold Tom Saji
B.Tech CSE · CEK · 2029

Hey! I'm Jerold Tom Saji, a first-year Computer Science & Engineering student at College of Engineering Kidangoor, Kerala. I'm someone who finds genuine excitement in understanding how things work — from the logic inside a microcontroller to the algorithms that power modern AI.

Right now I'm doing an industrial internship under the PMVIKAS programme at ITI Kottayam, where I'm getting hands-on with real engineering challenges — including building a self-balancing robot from scratch using Arduino and PID control, and designing a biodegradable coir helmet as a sustainable engineering experiment.

Outside labs and lectures, I love exploring the intersection of embedded systems and artificial intelligence — two fields that I think will define the next generation of technology. I'm always tinkering, learning, and pushing myself to build things that actually work in the real world.
